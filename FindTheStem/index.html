Find the stem, measure the trunk

Three real trees from a drone LiDAR cloud at Russell Sage WMA. Find each trunk in the understory slab, then measure its diameter at breast height the way the thesis did. The tape measurement is hidden until you commit.

How the thesis did it: a 0 to 3 m slab under each crown is drawn from above, the stem is marked, a 15 cm slice is cut at 1.37 m, and a circle is fitted to the ring of bark returns. On 122 clear trunks the fitted diameter landed about 2.5 cm from the tape on average.
